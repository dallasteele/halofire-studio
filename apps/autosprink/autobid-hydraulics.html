AutoBid · Remote-Area Hydraulic Calc (the Brady step, screening)
The binding remote-area calc, run over the real routed pipe network. The NFPA design density rides on the most-demanding (remote) operating heads; flow accumulates leaf → riser; Hazen-Williams friction (p = 4.52·Q1.85/(C1.85·d4.87), Schedule-40 internal bores) is summed on every segment of the demand path; looped mains are balanced with Hardy-Cross. Output: a converged demand gpm/psi vs the supply, with the per-segment loss path as the checkable number. Honest: this screens the bid — Brady's signed AutoSPRINK calc is the POST-AWARD binding number.
